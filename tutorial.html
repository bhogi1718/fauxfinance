Stock Market Basics

Here are some fundamental concepts:

  • Stock: A share in the ownership of a company.
  • Stock Exchange: A platform where stocks are bought and sold.
  • Stock Price: The current value of a stock.
  • Market Index: A measure of the overall stock market performance.
